List at $779,000. Target a sale around $750K.

Why $779,000 specifically.

  • It stays under the $780K psychological barrier. Buyers searching at $750K-$800K MLS bands include this listing. Buyers searching $800K+ start filtering for detached homes — a fight a townhouse can’t win.
  • It positions you about 3% above the same-complex sale. 39 Panabaker #1 sold for $760K in Dec 2024. Your list price reflects 16 months of stability, your renovation, and the walkout — without overreaching.
  • It allows for normal negotiation. Today’s buyers expect 3-5% off list. A $779K list landing at $745-755K is a clean transaction; a $799K list landing at $745K feels like a fire sale.
  • It avoids the price-drop spiral. Properties listed at $799-$849K in this segment are cancelling, expiring, and re-listing across multiple cycles. Pricing right the first time gets you sold; pricing high gets you stale.
  • It positions you above — but in conversation with — the eight active competitors. The active set tops out at $725K. At $779K we’re asking the buyer for a $50–80K premium, which the renovation, walkout, and sold-comp data all support — provided the home shows accordingly and the listing leads with those features.

Section Nine · The Six Things to Take Away

The full picture, on one page.

  • You bought in May 2022 at $780K — peak-of-market territory. The benchmark hit $1.07M that month and was below $830K within five months. You bought at a difficult time, and the math is what it is.
  • Today’s benchmark is $785K. The market has stabilized in a $770K-$900K band over the last three years. Your home, properly priced, sits squarely inside that band.
  • The most relevant comparable is 39 Panabaker #1 — same complex, end unit, fully renovated — which sold for $760,000 in December 2024. That sale is your anchor.
  • Five 2022 buyers in your immediate market have lost between $55K and $180K when forced to sell. The average is $115,000. Your projected $30K result is a fraction of that.
  • Eight townhouses are actively listed within a few kilometres at $615–$725K. Most are sitting with reductions already taken. Your home will be priced above this set — and the renovation, walkout, and same-complex anchor are what justify it. The listing has to lead with those features.
  • List $779,000. Target a sale around $750,000. Stay under the $780K line so the home sells against townhouses, not detached. Take the small loss now rather than letting the home sit.
