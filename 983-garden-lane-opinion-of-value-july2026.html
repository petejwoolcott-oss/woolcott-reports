Outbuilding Contribution to Value

Rural buyers generally discount outbuilding values relative to their construction cost — maintenance, insurance, and utility obligations transfer with the property. The following estimates reflect market contribution, not replacement cost.

Workshop 1 — 25×30 ft (750 sqft) — Heated + Power

$60,000 – $80,000

Heated and powered — the most functional and versatile structure. Includes party room, 2-pc bathroom, wet bar, and deck with above-ground pool. Effectively a secondary recreation and entertainment space.

Workshop 2 — 25×30 ft (750 sqft) — Power Only

$20,000 – $30,000

Powered but unheated — suited for storage, seasonal use, or hobbyist workspace. Solid functional value for rural buyers with equipment, vehicles, or materials storage needs.

Quonset Building

$10,000 – $20,000

Provides additional covered storage capacity. Exact contribution dependent on size, condition, and whether it has power. Valued conservatively pending inspection of structure.

Above-Ground Pool

$5,000 – $8,000

Considered personal property in most jurisdictions. As a fixture attached to the deck it has minimal real property value. Seasonal asset — limited year-round utility in Ontario climate.
