Section 5

Key Takeaways & Recommended Action

  • This is an active market — sellers are achieving 96–98% of ask. Waterfront units at 56 Annie Craig sold at 96–98% of their final list prices within 23–50 days, and non-waterfront comps moved even faster (12–14 DOM). Don't expect a deep discount, but there is room to negotiate below asking.
  • Waterfront vs. non-waterfront is the single biggest pricing factor. Waterfront/water-view units in this community are trading at roughly $940–$980/sqft, while non-waterfront units land at $730–$835/sqft. Unit 207 is non-waterfront (Waterfront Y/N: No per MLS), meaning the asking price of $798/sqft is at the upper boundary of fair value for its category.
  • The seller tried to lease this unit first — and failed. The lease listing ($3,100/mo) was terminated after just ~20 days in March 2026. This signals motivation to sell quickly and may give you modest negotiating leverage. The pivot from lease to sale in under a week suggests the seller has made a decision and wants to move.
  • The unit is vacant and available — that has real value. A 60–90 day flexible closing on a vacant unit means you can take possession quickly, rent immediately, or move in without the hassle of coordinating with a tenant. This is a genuine positive for the seller's position.
  • The strongest comparable is 608-110 MP Dr (same building, 2 bed/2 bath, non-WF, 900 sqft), which sold in October 2025 for $660,000 ($733/sqft). Your unit at 843 sqft with SW exposure is slightly smaller but arguably better positioned — fair value lands between those figures.
  • Based on the sold data, comparable $/sqft, and current market conditions, the data supports an offer in the range of $640,000 – $660,000. An offer at $650,000 represents approximately 96% of ask — in line with recent SP/LP ratios in this community — and should be taken seriously by the seller. Going in at full ask ($674,000) is not supported by the non-waterfront comp data.
