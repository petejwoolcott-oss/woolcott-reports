The Problem With $1,870,000+

At $1.87M+, the subject competes with fresh listings and no stigma. Buyers who saw the previous three attempts are not coming back at the same price. The only way to generate new showings is to undercut the competition meaningfully.

The Opportunity

Priced at $1,799,000–$1,849,000, this is the best-value 5-bedroom in the pocket. Buyers actively comparing 4-bed homes near $1.6M will stretch for the extra bedroom. The Daniel Way and Post Road 4-beds all cleared between $1.66M–$1.96M.

The Stigma Factor

Buyers Have a Long Memory.
History Follows This Address.

Every buyer's agent who pulls 66 Merlin Street sees the full history: listed at $2.1M in July 2025, still unsold 10 months later. That history signals one of two things to a buyer:

  • The sellers are unrealistic — negotiations will be difficult
  • Something is wrong with the property that isn't showing in the listing

Neither is true — but perception is reality. The only way to break through this stigma is a price that makes buyers stop and think: "this is actually a deal."

Going back at $1,870,000 or higher puts the property right back where it failed. The market has already spoken at that price — loudly and repeatedly.
