Price Recommendation & Verdict

Recommended List Price
$799,900
Expected Sale Range: $780,000 – $810,000
The current list price of $799,900 is well-supported by market evidence. It sits $50,000 above the most recent conditional sale of a similar-sized renovated home without a garage or legal suite — and at the same price point as 16 Ronaldshay Ave, a legal duplex with smaller sqft and no garage. The $799,900 price is neither aggressive nor conservative; it accurately reflects 114 Luscombe's rare asset profile in this market.
Is $799K the Right Price? Yes. The data confirms this position. 201 Ridge (no garage, not legal) went conditional at $749,900 — adding the garage (~$22,000) and legal duplex premium (~$20,000–$25,000) brings the indicated value to ~$795,000–$797,000, squarely supporting $799,900. The only active competition at the same price (16 Ronaldshay) is inferior on both garage and sqft. There is no evidence supporting a higher price: 74 Victor Blvd at $829,900 (legal, larger, 2-car garage) has been sitting for 84 days. $799,900 is the correct number.

Positioning Recommendations

  • 1
    Lead with Legal Duplex Status in All Marketing Buyers and investors actively searching for income properties will filter specifically for legal duplex. Ensure MLS remarks, social content, and feature sheets all prominently reference the registered legal duplex status and $4,650/month prior rental income. This is the property's most powerful differentiator.
  • 2
    Highlight the 2021 Architect-Designed Renovation The full gut renovation designed by an architect in 2021 speaks to both quality and compliance — especially important for a legal duplex where structural and electrical integrity matters to buyers. Make this a talking point in all representations.
  • 3
    Showcase the Metal Roof as a Long-Term Asset A metal roof has a 40–70 year lifespan, versus 15–20 years for asphalt shingles. This is a significant capital expenditure that buyers won't need to plan for, and it differentiates the home from active competition where roofs are aging or recently replaced.
  • 4
    Target Both Owner-Occupants and Investors The property serves two distinct buyer profiles: families seeking an in-law suite or mortgage helper, and investors seeking a turn-key income property with reliable tenancy potential. Tailor listing copy and social targeting to reach both audiences.
  • 5
    Monitor 201 Ridge Street's Close Price 201 Ridge Street (conditional at $749,900) is expected to close in the coming weeks. Its final confirmed sale price will be the clearest market data point to date for this property segment. If it closes close to asking, it further validates the $799,900 positioning for the subject.
