02
Bungalow on Nugent Dr Is a Rare Product
Only two bungalow sales in recent data — both smaller than the subject and both sold in under 17 days. Buyers seeking single-floor living in Kentley have very limited options. The subject's 1,700 sqft AG footprint, open concept layout, and oversized primary bedroom position it as the premium bungalow available.
03
Lead with the Mortgage Helper Story
$2,200–$2,300/mo in potential rental income ($27K/yr) is a compelling owner-occupier story. Market as "in-law suite" or "income potential" — not as a legal rental unit. Buyers will factor this into affordability calculations, widening the qualified buyer pool significantly.
04
Stands Out Against Backsplit Competition
All five active competitors are backsplits or multi-level homes. The subject's single-floor living differentiates it for buyers who want step-free main floor access — downsizers, retirees, and those with mobility considerations. These buyers are specifically searching for a bungalow and will not be distracted by multi-level alternatives.
05
Condition and Finishes Justify the Premium
The comparable 135 Nugent (same street, basic finish, no suite) sold for $590K. The subject's updated condition — pot lights, hardwood floors, ceramics, open-concept main floor — plus the income suite justifies a $160K premium above that comp. Ensure the presentation reflects this: professional photos, staged main floor, clean suite entrance.
06
Expected Close: $725K – $750K
At $749,900, neighbourhood SP/LP ratios of 0.96–1.00 point to a close range of $720K–$750K. The fresh listing with competitive pricing should generate early showings. Market to two buyer profiles: (1) downsizer/retiree seeking main-floor bungalow; (2) owner-occupier seeking income suite to offset carrying costs.
