Five transactions closed in the Burlington and Hamilton-area rural market over the past 60 days, with sold prices ranging from $2,400,000 to $2,620,000. The ceiling of that range is meaningful: despite strong property offerings across Carlisle, Burlington NEC, and Flamborough, no comparable sold above $2,620,000 in the period.

The most directly relevant comparable is 15 Palomino Drive, Carlisle. At 7,474 SF total and 6 bedrooms on a 1-acre lot, it is the closest match to the subject in terms of scale, and it sold quickly — 21 days on market — at $2,400,000. The SP/LP ratio of 0.92 reflects a modest but typical negotiation discount on a premium property, not distressed pricing. This sale establishes the lower boundary of the subject's probable value range.

The two Burlington NEC sales (Cedar Springs and Breckenridge) are materially smaller homes (~3,400 SF AG) but confirm buyers are active and willing to transact in this market at the $2.4M–$2.6M level. On a per-square-foot basis these homes traded at $719–$754/SF above grade, compared to approximately $351/SF for the subject at current list — reflecting the well-documented discount buyers apply to larger luxury homes where incremental square footage carries diminishing per-foot value.

The active market tells a clear story about where buyers are transacting and where they are not. Three properties have gone conditional — spanning $2,300,000 to $2,699,000 — but the two lower-priced conditionals resolved quickly, while Chesswood accumulated 355 cumulative days before finding a buyer at $2,300,000.

At the upper end of active inventory, 2129 Highview Drive in Burlington is listed at $2,689,000 and has been on market for 81 days without an accepted offer. This property directly overlaps with the subject's price territory and its inability to attract a conditional offer reinforces that buyer resistance intensifies above $2,650,000.

The cluster of active listings between $2,349,000 and $2,450,000 represents the most competitive price band in the current market. In aggregate, the active market confirms what the solds indicate: buyer activity is concentrated in the $2,300,000–$2,500,000 range. Properties priced above $2,600,000 are accumulating market time without offers. The subject's current list price of $2,650,000 places it above the demonstrated point of buyer engagement.
