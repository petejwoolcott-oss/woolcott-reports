Good to know

Wheels required Saturday

The loop needs a car (~3.5 hrs total driving, all scenic). No rental? Half-day bus tours to Peggy's Cove leave from downtown.

Lock in dinner now

The Bicycle Thief books up on weekends — call +1 902-425-7993 once the dates are set.

Feeling fancy?

Tribute, on the waterfront, does a chef's-counter tasting menu that's become one of the best meals in Atlantic Canada. The splurge-night move.

Wine people?

Swap a half-day for Wolfville & the Annapolis Valley (~1 hr the other direction) — sparkling and Tidal Bay tastings at spots like Benjamin Bridge.

History hit

The Halifax Citadel fires its noon cannon daily, with great harbour views from the ramparts. Easy add before the Sunday market.

Real talk at Peggy's Cove

It's colder and windier than town, so bring a layer — and stay off the dark, wet rocks near the water. Rogue waves are no joke.
