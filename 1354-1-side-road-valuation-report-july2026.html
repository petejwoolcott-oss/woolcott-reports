Market Context — North Burlington Rural, 60-Day Window

The last 60 days produced eight comparable transactions across the North Burlington Rural / Flamborough corridor, establishing a clear market ceiling and buyer profile. Properties in the NEC Dev Control area (well/septic, rural acreage) are transacting in the $1.95M–$2.6M range, with the higher end reserved for either exceptional luxury builds (37 Appaloosa Trail in Carlisle: custom 2002, saltwater pool + cabana, sold $2,555,000 in 16 days) or large land-play purchases (6101 Walkers Line: 26 acres of escarpment land, sold $2,597,000 despite a modest 1,895 sqft home). For comparable-era homes — renovated or updated, well/septic, rural but modest lot sizes — buyers are settling in the $2.04M–$2.35M range.

The active competition provides useful context for pricing decisions. 6115 Guelph Line (listed June 22 at $2,349,000 — a converted 1860 barn with 5,673 sqft AG on 4 acres) and 1065 Milburough Line (conditional at $2,499,999 — sold in 34 days) both offer more acreage than the subject. The subject's distinction is different but equally compelling: at 7,563 total finished sqft — 4,726 above grade — it offers significantly more usable living space than any comparable in the market. For the right buyer, space, functionality, and a self-contained in-law suite are the defining features, and this property delivers all three.

At the current price, the blended cost is $324/total sqft — a genuine value story for a buyer who understands what that square footage means in daily living. The three prior offers across the listing period confirm that buyers have recognized this value. The task ahead is reaching the next one at the right moment, at the right price, with fresh eyes on the property.

Path Forward — Six Actions to Maximize Your Position
1
Launch Fresh at $2,199,000 with a New MLS Number
A deliberate re-launch — new MLS number, new photography, fresh remarks — gives the property a genuine restart with the buyer pool. Three prior offers confirm that buyers respond to this home; the goal is to reach the next serious buyer before they commit elsewhere. At $2,199,000, the subject undercuts every active competitor by at least $100,000 while remaining close to the supported market value of $2,250,000. Position the re-launch proactively to the brokerage community: "newly priced, must be seen." This is a confident, informed move — not a reaction, but a strategy.
2
Lead With the Multigenerational Story
The subject's most differentiated and time-relevant feature is the fully self-contained east wing in-law suite — its own kitchen, bathroom, living and bedroom with no shared interior access required. This is the feature that no other active comparable can match, and it speaks directly to the fastest-growing buyer profile in the Burlington area: families looking to house aging parents or adult children. The MLS remarks, all photography, and digital advertising should open with this message, not with square footage or the golf course.
3
Set an Offer Presentation Date (Day 14)
Fresh-launch the property with an offer presentation date 14 days out, clearly communicated in MLS. This creates urgency for buyers who have been watching, eliminates the "I'll think about it" pattern, and signals genuine seller intent. The 7,563 sqft value story at $291/sqft is compelling enough to draw multiple parties if they are all in the room at the same time. If no offers materialize on presentation day, the listing remains active but the urgency window will have produced 10+ showings and meaningful feedback.
4
New Photography — Lifestyle Focus on Covered Deck
The covered outdoor deck with gas fireplace is this property's best answer to the "no pool" objection. Current listing photography likely reflects the property from years of different campaigns. Commission a fresh shoot (ideally at dusk/golden hour) that makes the covered deck look like a 12-month outdoor living room — fire lit, furniture staged, lights on. Buyers comparing this home to 37 Appaloosa Trail ($2.555M, saltwater pool + cabana) need to see an outdoor lifestyle vision, not an empty concrete pad.
5
Tell the 2021 Renovation Story Prominently
Electric, plumbing, furnace, and roof all replaced in 2021 — this means the subject's ownership cost profile is closer to a new build than a 1960 structure. Most buyers overestimate the maintenance risk on a 60-year-old home and discount accordingly. The MLS remarks and feature sheet should lead with this clearly: "Rebuilt from the inside out — 100% of major systems replaced 2021." The goal is to neutralize the age objection before the showing, not after.
6
Target Agent Outreach — 5km Radius
A direct agent-to-agent communication reaching the 20–30 most active agents in the Waterdown/Burlington North/Carlisle market is a high-value step at any stage of the campaign. Framing it around the fresh launch — "newly priced, must be seen, offer date in 14 days" — gives co-operating agents a clear reason to revisit this property with their buyer clients. Including the value per sqft comparison ($291/total sqft vs $415–$805 for comparable active listings) gives those agents a compelling data point. Woolcott's strong market share in this corridor makes this a warm, relationship-first outreach — not a cold call.
