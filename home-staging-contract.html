The Staging Company and Client may be individually referred to as "Party" and collectively as the "Parties".

2. Definitions

For the purpose of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings set forth below:

2.1 Home Staging

The process of preparing a residential property for sale in the real estate marketplace by enhancing its appeal to potential buyers.

2.2 Staging Company

The business entity providing professional home staging services as detailed in this Agreement.

2.3 Client/Homeowner

The individual or entity hiring the Staging Company to stage their property.

2.4 Property

The residential property subject to staging as specified in this Agreement, located at:

2.5 Duration

The period for which staging services will be provided, including setup, maintenance, and retrieval of staging furnishings.

3. Agreement Overview
3.1 Purpose

The Staging Company agrees to provide professional home staging services for the Client's Property with the intention of enhancing its marketability and appeal to prospective buyers.

3.2 Scope of Service

The Staging Company shall furnish, arrange, and style the Property with furniture, artwork, accessories, and other decorative items as deemed appropriate by the Staging Company's professional judgment and in accordance with the specifications outlined in this Agreement. Walkways and/or driveways are to be accessible by the client for our stagers upon arrival.

3.3 Consult

A consult will be a 30-minute up to 1-hour walkthrough of the home with the lead stager. The lead stager will take pictures of every room and send them back with recommendations for each room. If applicable, the homeowner is to ensure the staging company has elevator access during stage time.

3.4 Services Do Not Include

Staging services do not include sourcing of vanities, lighting, flooring, drapes, runners, carpet, counters, etc. If these services are wanted, there will be an extra charge of $100 per hour billed by the lead stager directly to the client. Staging services also do not include the removal or storage of items, nor rearranging furniture back to original condition after removal. If there is a last-minute cancellation by the client, the client will be placed on a wait list for the next available time.

4. Services Provided
4.1 Staging Design

The Staging Company shall implement a design approach that considers the Property's architectural features, target market demographics, and current market trends.

4.2 Setup and Breakdown Schedule

4.2.1 Setup Date: The Staging Company shall commence staging on , 20 and complete setup by , 20.

4.2.2 Duration: The staging items shall remain at the Property for the duration of this listing term.

4.2.3 Breakdown: The Staging Company shall remove all staging items within business days after the termination date of this Agreement or upon written notice from the Client indicating the Property has been sold or is no longer being marketed for sale, whichever occurs first.

5. Compensation / Commission

5.1 Commission: The listing side commission for staging must be at least 2.25%.

6. Client Responsibilities
6.1 Access to Property

The Client shall provide the Staging Company with access to the Property during setup, staging, and breakdown periods, including keys, access codes, or other necessary means of entry.

6.2 Property Condition

Prior to staging, the Property must be:

  • Clean and free from debris
  • Adequately cleared of personal items to accommodate staging furniture and accessories
  • Utilizing all suggestions implemented during the consultation
6.3 Communication

The Client agrees to promptly respond to communications from the Staging Company and provide timely feedback throughout the staging process.

7. Staging Company Obligations
7.1 Quality Assurance

The Staging Company commits to utilizing quality furnishings and décor items that are in good condition, clean, and appropriate for the Property and target market.

7.2 Insurance

The Staging Company maintains liability insurance coverage in the amount of for damages that may occur during the staging process which are directly attributed to the negligence of the Staging Company or its representatives.

8. Termination & Pet Fees
8.1 Termination Conditions

8.1.1 Staging Recovery Clause: In the event the Listing Agreement is cancelled by the Seller prior to the expiry date, and the property was staged by Woolcott Real Estate, the Seller agrees to reimburse the Brokerage for labour and associated staging expenses as follows:

  • A) Accessories Only: Where staging consists solely of accessories and décor items — reimbursement of $1,000.
  • B) Any Other Staging Items: Where staging includes any furniture or items other than accessories and décor — reimbursement of $2,000.

8.1.2 Once reimbursement has been received, the cancellation will be processed.

8.1.3 Pet-Friendly: The Client agrees to ensure that all pets are supervised and restricted from climbing on or damaging staged items. The Client assumes full responsibility for any damages, stains, odors, or excessive cleaning required as a result of pets during the staging period. Any such damage will be repaired, replaced, or cleaned at the Client's expense, and the Staging Company reserves the right to invoice the Client accordingly.

9. Liability
9.1 Limitations

The Staging Company shall not be liable for:

  • Pre-existing damage to the Property
  • Normal wear and tear to the Property
  • Damage caused by third parties not under the Staging Company's control
  • Indirect, consequential, or incidental damages
9.2 Client Liability

The Client assumes responsibility for any damage to staging items caused by the Client, the Client's family members, guests, and pets. If staging pieces are damaged or not returned, the client will be invoiced.

10. Miscellaneous
10.1 Amendments

Any amendments or modifications to this Agreement must be made in writing and signed by both Parties.

10.2 Dispute Resolution

In the event of any dispute arising out of or relating to this Agreement, the Parties agree to first attempt to resolve the dispute through good-faith negotiation. If negotiation fails, the Parties agree to submit the dispute to mediation before pursuing litigation.

10.3 Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for any failure or delay in performance due to circumstances beyond their reasonable control, including but not limited to acts of God, natural disaster, pandemic, war, terrorism, or civil unrest.

10.4 Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, or negotiations.

10.5 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, such provision shall be struck and the remaining provisions shall be enforced.

10.6 Assignment

Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party.
