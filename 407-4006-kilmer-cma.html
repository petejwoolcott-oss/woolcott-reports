Reading the days-on-market

Across the fourteen competitors the average time on market is ~59 days, but it splits cleanly in two. The units that launched at realistic prices are moving — 2040 Cleaver #206 at 11 days, Palmer at 12, and three more fresh listings at 31–32 days. The units that started high are bleeding: 3499 Upper Middle (136 days, −$63K), 1411 Walkers #202 (118 days, −$60K), 5220 Dundas #508 (115 days, −$26K) and #D422 (192 cumulative days). The subject — 45 days in, one $28,900 cut already behind it, still unsold — is sitting on the on-ramp to that second group. Holding at $470,000 against eight cheaper, often better-equipped rivals is how a listing drifts from 45 days to 120.

Valuation Analysis

What it's worth — and how to price for 30 days

Anchoring to the closest comparables: the one-bathroom cohort traded between $494 and $580 per square foot, with the best size-match (2085 Appleby, 921 sq ft) at $520/sq ft. Smaller units carry higher per-foot figures, so the right anchor for an 890 sq ft unit is the low-$500s.

At 890 sq ft, applying the size-matched $520/sq ft yields about $463,000. The subject earns a modest premium for being top-floor with 9-ft ceilings, a brand-new 2024 furnace and A/C, and the lowest condo fee in the entire comparable set ($451.74) — that fee advantage alone saves a buyer $1,800–$8,000 per year versus most comps and is a genuine, marketable differentiator. Against that, it carries the two clearest discounts in this market: only one bathroom and a single parking spot, where nearly every unit that closed above $480K had two baths.

Netting those out, fair market value lands in the $455,000–$472,000 range, with the most probable sale price around $460,000–$465,000. At $470,000 the subject is asking slightly above the size-matched benchmark — defensible, but not a bargain, which is exactly why 45 days have passed without an offer.

Recommendation · 30-Day Strategy
Reposition at $459,900 to sell inside the window.

Dropping under the $460K threshold captures the large buyer pool searching below $460,000, signals sharp value against the higher-fee 2-bath comps, and creates urgency — the conditions every fast sale in this set shared (15–30 days). It also moves the subject out from above the average competitor and into the active, moving part of the market: at $459,900 it would sit ahead of only the few stale, deeply-cut holdouts rather than behind eight cheaper rivals. Expect a sale in the $458,000–$465,000 range. Holding at $470,000 is defensible on value but, with fourteen live competitors and eight of them cheaper, risks the slow grind the 115- to 192-day actives are living through right now.
