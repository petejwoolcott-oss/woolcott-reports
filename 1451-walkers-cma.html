Valuation Analysis

What it's worth — and what it takes to sell

The tightest anchor is 1441 Walkers #311 — same complex, same vintage, 1,026 sq ft — which closed at $468/sq ft. Applied to 1,020 sq ft, that yields $477,200. Adjusting down for the subject's higher fees and absence of a gas fireplace, and adjusting up for the 2024 furnace/AC and new washer, the net lands in the mid-to-high $460s. The 47-day market exposure confirms the calculus: the price hasn't found a buyer yet.

The subject and 1441 Walkers #311 are as close to twins as the data provides — same HCP 343, same 1998 vintage, nearly identical square footage (1,020 vs 1,026 sq ft). The 1441 unit sold at $480,000 after 103 days — and that was with a gas fireplace and a $45/month cheaper condo fee. Strip those two advantages and the natural landing zone for the subject is slightly below $480K, not at it.

The positive offsets are real: a 2024 updated furnace and A/C (same age as the 1441 unit's furnace, per that listing), a brand-new washer with warranty, and the Kitec replacement already done. Buyers won't pay a significant premium for these — they're expected maintenance items at this price point — but they remove objections and prevent price chipping during a home inspection.

Factoring the comp data, the supported market value range is $455,000 to $480,000, with the most probable transaction in the $462,000–$472,000 band. The current $479,900 list is at the top of the range — defensible on paper, but the 47 days of market exposure says it isn't where buyers are comfortable landing.

The Bottom Line

Key takeaways

  • 1 The best comp is in the same complex — and it took 103 days to sell. 1441 Walkers #311 in the same HCP 343 complex, nearly the same size, sold at $480,000 in April 2026 — but only after a relaunch that masked a 103-day total marketing cycle. The subject is asking $100 less, but lacks the fireplace and carries $45/month in higher fees. The market is consistent: this is $460–$480K product.
  • 2 The nearby corridor reinforces a cautionary price story. 1411 Walkers #202 (900 sq ft) launched at $519,900, spent 123 days on market, and closed at $450,000 after $69,900 in cuts. The Tansley/Walkers buyer pool knows this market and waits for the discount. Pricing ahead of that expectation adds days, not dollars.
  • 3 $479,900 sits at the ceiling of what the data supports. At $470/sq ft the subject is right at — or just above — the $468/sq ft that the closest comp achieved. Without a fireplace, with higher fees, and at 47 DOM, the market is already pricing in a discount. A proactive adjustment is more effective than a reactive one.
  • 4 The updates are real but priced in. The 2024 furnace/AC, new washer, and replaced Kitec are excellent selling points and remove buyer objections during inspection — but they're maintenance items, not renovations. Buyers at this price point factor them as expected, not exceptional. The $590K Appleby comp shows what $542/sq ft requires: quartz, hardwood, updated cabinetry throughout.
  • 5 The right move: $469,900. A $10K reduction crosses the psychological search break at $470K, creates fresh momentum, and targets a realistic close of $458,000–$465,000. The seller gives up very little on paper — the comps don't support the last $10K anyway — and gains a deal. Recommended expected sale price at $469,900 list: $458,000–$465,000.
