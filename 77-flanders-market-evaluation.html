The 1,000 sq ft Accessory Dwelling Unit is the story of this listing. A legal, fully permitted two-storey addition — all 1,000 sq ft of it above grade — with one bedroom, a full bathroom, and a complete kitchen, connected to the main home on both floors. It works as a fully private in-law suite, a multigenerational wing, or potential income space, while remaining integrated enough to live as one large home. Almost nothing else in Waterdown's current or recent inventory offers this, and because it is permitted and above grade (not a basement conversion), it materially widens the buyer pool and supports the price premium with documentation in hand.

Summary

Key Takeaways

  • $1,999,900 is the recommended list price. It is supported by the $1,840,000 sale of the near-identical 116 Grindstone Way plus a defensible premium for the 1,000 sq ft ADU, larger above-grade area, and Bruce Trail conservancy backing.
  • No comparable home on a standard lot has closed above $1,840,000 in the past 400 days. The lone sale above that — 342 Mountain Brow at $2,300,000 — was a 1.7-acre estate with a pool, and even it settled $195,000 below ask. Pricing at the top of the range ($2,049,900) would rely on features carrying the day against no direct closed-sale support — a risk this market is actively punishing.
  • Flanders Drive itself is proving where the ceiling is. 81 Flanders has accumulated 150 days at $2,399,000 and 9 Flanders was cancelled at $2,299,900 after 64 days. At $1,999,900, 77 Flanders is $300,000+ below both — a compelling value story against larger, more updated homes.
  • Overpricing costs real money here. The average sale closed at 94.5% of final list; three of the four sellers gave up $195,000–$257,000 from their original asking prices, while sharply priced 136 Grindstone sold in 24 days at 98.9% of ask.
  • Timing: the September 14 launch means competing head-to-head with 9 Flanders. The listing agent confirms it relists at a reduced price after Labour Day, so expect it live — likely around $2.2M — when 77 Flanders hits the market. That makes the $1,999,900 position even more important: buyers comparing the two on the same street will see 77 Flanders at $200,000+ less, under the $2M search threshold, with a legal ADU that 9 Flanders can't match. A mid-September launch also catches the fall market at full strength, with buyers re-engaged after summer.
  • The ADU is the differentiator — and it's legal, permitted, and fully above grade. A self-contained 1,000 sq ft two-storey suite with full kitchen is nearly unique in current Waterdown inventory. Marketing should lead with the permits in hand — a documented legal ADU commands a premium a basement conversion never could.
  • Under $2,000,000 matters mechanically. $1,999,900 keeps the listing visible in both the $1.5–2M and around-$2M search bands, maximizing exposure at launch — when it counts most.
