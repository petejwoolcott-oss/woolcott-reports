Every active competitor has lingering history — 73 Arkell started at $999K, 84 South Oval at $949K, 33 Cline at $869K. The subject comes to market fresh with no prior DOM. In Westdale, where buyers are savvy and track pricing history, a clean first listing with a well-supported price is a significant competitive advantage. Lead with that.
03
2-pc Bathroom: Rough-In Confirmed, Proceed
Rough-in is confirmed and the location is off the kitchen — the best possible placement. At ~$5K for fixtures and labour, the 2-pc adds an estimated $10–15K in market value (2–3× ROI) and enables the listing to market as "1+1 baths," removing the single-bath objection entirely. This is one of the cleanest pre-listing investments possible for this property.
04
Furnace / HWT: Disclose and Price In
The 2005 furnace and hot water tank are 21 years old — buyers and their inspectors will flag this. Proactive disclosure in the listing is preferable to a renegotiation post-offer. Either budget a credit of $3,000–$5,000 in negotiation, or replace the HWT (relatively low cost) prior to listing to reduce the objection.
05
Westdale Buyers Want Move-In Ready
The 8-day sale of 68 Bond St at $889K and the 11-day sale of 9 Barclay at $885K confirm that well-presented, ready-to-occupy Westdale homes move fast. Professional staging and photography are essential — this neighbourhood attracts buyers who will pay premium for minimal work. The 2021 kitchen and updated finishes are the story; let photos tell it.
06
Expected Close: $825K – $855K
With the 2-pc added and listing at $864,900, SP/LP ratios of 0.96–1.00 point to a close range of $825K–$855K. The 1+1 bath configuration moves 89 Barclay out of the single-bath tier and into direct competition with the $870–$889K sold comps — a meaningful shift in buyer perception that supports the higher close range.
