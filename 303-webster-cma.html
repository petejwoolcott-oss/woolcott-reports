Since We Last Spoke

The March 2025 estimate, revisited honestly

In March 2025 we valued 303 Webster at $1,100,000–$1,175,000, and that was the right number for that market. Roughly sixteen months later, the evidence says Hamilton East has softened — not collapsed, but repriced — and an honest update matters more than a comfortable one. Three tiers — and 303 Webster belongs in the top one

The Bottom Line

Key takeaways for 303 Webster Road

  • The March 2025 range of $1,100,000–$1,175,000 no longer holds, and the honest revision is $1,000,000–$1,050,000. That ~9–11% adjustment matches the HPI benchmark ($1,047K–$1,070K depending on baseline) and, more importantly, matches what actually closed.
  • The market is thin: only 16 detached 2-storey sales in Hamilton 28 in the past 365 days. With few buyers active at this price point, the cost of missing the first weeks of exposure is higher than in a normal market.
  • Pricing at 2025 levels has a documented price tag. 136 Country Club ($1.36M ask, April 2025 → $1,065,000, 230 days) and 100 National ($1.379M → $1,070,000, 209 days) each surrendered roughly $300,000 and seven-plus months chasing the market down. The comp set averaged 94.8% of final list but only 89.5% of original ask — the gap is the overpricing tax.
  • 72 National Drive is the blueprint: ravine lot, inground pool, no rear neighbours, listed at $1,099,900 in April 2026 — conditional in 4 days, closed at $1,100,888, 100% of ask, in 11 days. Premium-lot homes still command premium prices when the ask respects today’s market.
  • The lot is doing the earning in this market. The 4,029 sq ft home at 1012 Greenhill brought $898,000 on a conventional lot, while 72 National’s 2,775 sq ft brought $1,100,888 on ravine. 303 Webster’s Escarpment backing, dead-end street, pool and recent upgrades put it firmly on the premium side of that divide.
  • Three of the seven actives — the three priciest — are Power of Sale files with confirmed slow showings. 43 Riesling ($1,095,000, 246 combined days), 7 Chardonnay ($1,149,900) and 27 Riesling ($1,189,000, deal collapsed in June) are motivated, as-is, lender-driven listings. They are not evidence of value, and buyers know it.
  • Recommended approach: list at $999,900 with strong presentation and let the property’s scarcity work. A turnkey, honestly-priced Escarpment-backing pool home would be the only compelling non-distressed option above $1M in Gershome today — the conditions that produce results at the top of the $1.00M–$1.05M range, and the same playbook that just worked on National Drive.
