Market Summary — Flamborough Rural Segment

The Flamborough rural market (sub-areas 040 Greensville, 043 Flamborough West, 044 Flamborough East, 045 Beverly) remains active but is showing clear signs of price sensitivity. Properties that entered the market in Q4 2025 at peak-era pricing (e.g., 348 Sydenham at $1,329,900) have struggled, while well-priced, updated homes in Q1–Q2 2026 (56 Kirby at $1,375,000, 123 Hillcrest at $1,199,000, 15 Orkney at $1,175,000) are selling quickly at 0.98–1.03 SP/LP ratios with days-on-market in the single digits to low twenties.

The message is clear: price it right and it sells fast. Overprice it and it sits. Three of the current active listings have cumulative days-on-market of 33–193 days and have all dropped price at least once. Subject property has a genuinely differentiated offering (kitchen package, 3-car garage, farmland backing) that should generate real buyer interest — but needs to be priced to make the shortlist, not to anchor high.

Buyer profile in this segment: families and move-up buyers seeking more space and rural lifestyle while staying within 15–25 minutes of Hamilton, Burlington, and Waterdown. The 3-car garage and massive parking for 9–10 vehicles will resonate strongly with this demographic. The kitchen is a legitimate showstopper.
