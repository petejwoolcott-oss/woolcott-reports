System health

What's solid, what's fragile, and the open risks worth knowing before we pile on new features.

What's working
  • Command execution is gated. Nothing runs from a chat message without your Discord approval.
  • Credentials are out of reach. 36 secrets in an external store, none in files Henry reads or that sync to Drive.
  • Email & calendar need no tokens. They ride your always-on Outlook, so no more 90-day expiry outages.
  • Deal + appointment intel every Monday 7:30. Three deal stages, per-agent tallies, shoutouts.
  • Backups run nightly to D:. Full local disaster-recovery copy, plus a secrets copy.
What's fragile / open
  • Outlook must stay open. If it's closed or the PC sleeps, email/calendar reads pause. Stay on classic Outlook, not "new".
  • 4 tokens still need rotation. Exposed for months — reissue when you have 15 min.
  • Drive backup carries client PII. Tighten the sync so offer PDFs don't leave the machine.
  • App secret has a hard expiry. The deal tally leans on an Azure secret — needs a rotation reminder before it lapses.
  • Work Drive indexer still times out. SIGKILL on big batches; needs incremental indexing.

Security posture

Exec policy

Mode ask · allowlist for known scripts · inline-eval always prompts · approvals routed to your Discord DM · fallback deny. Henry can't edit its own guardrails — hard rule.

Channels

Teams requires @mention · display-name trust off · Discord approvers = you only. Loosening the allowlist happens organically as you approve real commands.
