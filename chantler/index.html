Section Five  ·  The Case for $1.3M

Five reasons the number holds.

  • The land is real and irreplaceable. 50.9 acres in Niagara with frontage of 2,521 ft. Rural A1 zoning land in this region trades at $10,000–$25,000+ per acre depending on use. Even at $10K/acre, the land alone is worth ~$510,000 before the house.
  • The house is 3,371 sq ft — fully updated and furnished. Comparable bungalows of 1,400–1,900 sqft are trading at $1.04M–$1.085M unfinished. A 3,371 sqft home fully turnkey with all contents included represents a meaningful size premium.
  • The STR licence has standalone value. Ontario municipalities have been restricting short-term rental licences since 2023. A valid, operating licence with 5 years of income history in a tourism-adjacent region (Niagara wine country) transfers with the property — this is not a speculative income projection, it's a documented operating business.
  • The farm lease offsets carry. $1,500/year is not transformative — but on a $1.3M acquisition, $1,500/year in passive income with no management overhead is a permanently positive entry in the carrying cost ledger.
  • The ask is already reasonable. The property was listed at $1.95M in 2019. It sold for $1.2M in 2021 after years of market resistance. The current sellers are not overreaching — the $1.35M ask represents a disciplined, market-aware starting position. $1.3M is a 3.7% reduction from ask — a normal, clean transaction.
"The last buyer paid $1.2M in 2021 — at peak market, with no improvements included. You're being asked for $1.35M after five years of capital investment, a fully furnished turnkey estate, and a licensed income stream. At $1.3M you're getting a better deal than the 2021 buyer did."
