The Property

A genuinely rare bungaloft on one of Aldershot South's most sought-after streets — directly across from Burlington Golf & Country Club. The home blends 1952 character (solid brick, hardwood, double-sided gas fireplace) with thoughtful modern upgrades including a chef's kitchen with 6-burner gas range, panoramic sunroom addition, and a spectacular top-floor primary retreat occupying the entire upper level with vaulted ceilings, dual walk-ins, and a 5-piece ensuite. The lot — 85 × 125 ft — and the oversized heated 2-car garage with 10-car parking are genuinely hard to replicate in this neighbourhood.

Buyer Considerations

What to Keep in Mind

  • ⚠️
    Crawl space / partial basement. Unlike most comps (full basements), 549 Ann has only a crawl space — limits usable square footage and storage. This is reflected in our adjusted values.
  • ⚠️
    1952 build — mechanical and structural unknowns. Budget for a thorough home inspection. Key items: foundation, HVAC, electrical (knob-and-tube risk), plumbing, and the sunroom addition integrity.
  • ℹ️
    677 King Rd sold at $2.195M — an outlier driven by a premium lot, full reno (2025), and heated saltwater pool. Not a comparable floor for 549 Ann Ave.
  • ℹ️
    877 Kingsway Drive sold 22% over list at $1.7M — suggests the seller was underpriced. Multi-generational/in-law suite property with 4,500 total sqft. Different buyer profile.
